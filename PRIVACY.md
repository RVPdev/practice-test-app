# Privacy Policy

**Last updated: September 12, 2026**

This Privacy Policy explains how the Practice Test App (the "App")
handles information. The short version: **the App does not collect,
transmit, or store any of your data on our servers, because it doesn't
have a server.** Everything you do in the App stays on your own device
or browser.

## What We Collect

**Nothing.** The App:

- Has no account system, sign-up, or login.
- Does not collect your name, email, or any other personal information.
- Does not use analytics, crash reporting, advertising, or tracking
  SDKs of any kind.
- Does not transmit question sets you create or import, your answers,
  or your attempt history anywhere. They never leave your device.

## Where Your Data Lives

Everything the App stores — bundled and imported question sets, sets
you build yourself, and your practice/mock attempt history — is saved
locally using your device's or browser's own storage (for example,
`AsyncStorage` on iOS/Android, or your browser's local storage on web).
It is:

- **Private to that device or browser.** It is not synced between your
  devices and is not visible to us or to anyone else.
- **Under your control.** You can delete individual sets and attempts
  in the App, or clear all App data through your device or browser's
  own settings (for example, clearing site data for the web build, or
  uninstalling the app on mobile).
- **Not recoverable by us.** Because we never receive it, we cannot
  back it up, restore it, or retrieve it for you if it's lost (e.g. by
  clearing browser data or uninstalling the app).

## Hosting Provider Logs

The web build is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like virtually any web host,
GitHub may automatically log standard technical request data (such as
IP address, browser type, and request timestamps) as part of operating
its infrastructure. That logging is performed by GitHub, not by us —
see [GitHub's own Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement)
for details. We do not have access to, and do not separately collect,
any such logs.

## Children's Privacy

The App does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone,
including children, because it does not collect personal information
from anyone. It is not directed at children under 13.

## Third-Party Content

Some bundled question sets reference publicly published exam objectives
from third-party certification providers (for example, CompTIA). This
Privacy Policy covers only the App itself — it does not cover, and we
are not responsible for, the privacy practices of any third-party
certification body, website, or service you may separately interact
with.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as the App changes. The "Last updated"
date above reflects the most recent revision. Since the App collects no
data, changes here are expected to be about clarifying what's already
true rather than introducing new data collection — but if that ever
changes, we'll update this policy to say so.
